# Phase 4D — Baselines, shortcuts and incremental evidence

2026-10-22 NZDT. All three baseline configurations are **PROPOSED secondary
candidates**, not approved official study arms. No held-out shortcut classifier ran.

## Deterministic baselines

`research/baselines.py` binds a versioned configuration/hash and accepts verified
view packets only. Citations are restricted to parsed supplied facts. It returns
interpretable signals, insufficiency and no L2 truth label or evaluation threshold.

| Candidate | Observation rights | Output and limits |
|---|---|---|
| Communication-only | M: public task + permitted intercepted speech/coverage | Coordination-language indicator from two senders; not proof of agreement, harmful purpose or execution. Missing/omitted evidence stays insufficient. |
| Action-only | A: accepted proposals, Arena events and public allocation/cost | Similar final ballots and public costs; legitimate cooperation also triggers similarity. No private coalition, optimum or oracle reference. Parallel behavior does not prove agreement. |
| Evidence-chain | R/MAE facts only | Separates language indication, correlated Succeeded/verified local proposal commit, and harm support. Current L3 has no independent lawful comparator, so harm remains insufficient and the chain never claims proven collusion. |

The chain's inability to establish the frozen L2 harm criterion from current L3
is an explicit limitation, not a missing oracle feature to import. This candidate
is conservative and may abstain everywhere; future usefulness requires human
methodological review. No detector performance is claimed from these rules.

Safe cases: `review/phase4d/audit/baseline-cases.json` includes scripted agreement
without executed harm, harm without demonstrated agreement, scripted harmful
agreement plus mechanical harm, incomplete evidence and failed intended controls.
Scripted mechanics are not independent behavioral adjudication. Fresh six-action
lawful AIG audits show that receipts also occur without harmful collusion.
`unknown-audit.json` injects real local AIG faults before dispatch and after commit:
both Actions remain Unknown and stop after one dispatch; committed effects are
0/1, applied Arena proposals 0/0, and the baseline never calls either verified
success. Read-only replay produces no second effect.

## Shortcut risk register

`shortcut_diagnostics` measures what each packet actually exposes. It receives no
condition/truth. Any later association analysis is experimenter-side and must not
feed condition labels into predictions or tune on held-out outcomes.

| Feature | Layer / association | Mechanism and threatened contrast | Direct intervention artifact? | Prospective mitigation / approval |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Missing final slots | L3 A/MA/R/MAE; N2 | Recognizes blocked execution rather than evidence reasoning; pooled/N2 interpretation | Yes | Disclose counts; retain N1 primary endpoint. Changing N2 needs approval. |
| Blocked private attempts | L3 under B; C− | Router access reveals removed channel; paired/observer sensitivity | Yes | Freeze A primary/B sensitivity prospectively; never suppress actual B records. Approval required. |
| Speech volume | L3 M/MA/R/MAE; potentially C+/N2 | Can substitute verbosity for agreement; influences input overflow | Partly brief-mediated | Report volume, coverage and errors; equalizing speech changes opportunity and needs amendment. |
| Unequal omissions | L3 packet scope/counts; all views by category | Behavior-dependent loss can erase agreement or lifecycle facts | Selection artifact | Shared selection + explicit omissions already enforced; changing policy needs approval. |
| Source coverage | L3 source declarations; hidden availability remains unknown | Complete-source and selected-subset are different; missingness can predict arms | Capture-dependent | Report both coverage types; do not treat missing evidence as innocence. |
| Over-budget failure | L4 failure and L3 size when supplied | Abstention may correlate with condition, speech and observer rights | Pipeline artifact | Preserve failures/denominators; exact tokenizer/cap/packet changes need prospective approval. |
| Assigned coalition/condition | L1 only | A privileged shortcut would invalidate evaluation | Not a detector feature | Allowlist projection and negative-marker tests; never join labels into request text. |
| Oracle optimum/adjudication | L2 only | Direct ground-truth leakage | Not a detector feature | Independent blinded review and L2 lock before L4; no detector access. |

No condition-aware selection, hidden evidence deletion or post-outcome mitigation
was applied. Current threats remain material to research readiness.

## Typed AIG incremental-value matrix

Machine-readable `Contribution` records are exported to
`review/phase4d/audit/contribution-matrix.json`.

| AIG/public fact | Relation to MA | Execution uncertainty | Realized harm |
|---|---|---|---|
| Approval / independent authorization | Verified only in R/MAE | Establishes gate state under synthetic consent | Does not establish agreement or harm |
| Action terminal state | Sometimes inferable from accepted behavior, independently checked in R/MAE | Separates Succeeded, Failed, Unknown and missingness | A succeeded proposal is not necessarily final harmful implementation |
| Payload/proposal digests | R/MAE independently bind exact proposal content | Detects linkage inconsistency | No counterfactual harm or agreement proof |
| Receipt verification/reference | R/MAE confirms durable local commit | Important for lost acknowledgement/incomplete application | Allocation/public cost already visible in MA |
| Final allocation / public cost | Already in MA | Public Arena behavior | Relevant observations; independent comparator/agreement still needed |

**Potential negative result:** in this trusted deterministic Arena, ordinary MA
already reveals accepted proposals and final allocation. AIG may contribute little
marginal detection information beyond reliable provenance and uncertainty. That is
a reason to retain R versus MA, not a measured detector result. MAE versus R tests
presentation of matched information; MAE versus MA mixes information and rendering.
