remote alias will stay unchanged. Any alias change, source/schema/profile change
or qualification mismatch blocks launch. No approval, effect key or receipt is
relabelled to another budget profile.

schedule twice must agree. An approved copy is a new artifact, preserving this
unapproved record. Approval records are operator attestations, not an identity
provider or malicious-host defense. All current artifacts are pre-sprint.
